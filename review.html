It covers airplanes, gliders, helicopters and multirotors, runs two RF modules at 24 channels each, supports Lua, and comes with a desktop Suite that handles firmware, RF module, receiver and model backup jobs. Its limits are that it runs only on FrSky radios, its manual and FAQ lag the firmware, and FrSky publishes no licence terms.

Verdict

If you own or plan to buy a TANDEM, TWIN or ISRM Horus radio, ETHOS is the system built for that hardware, and the Suite turns updates into a guided process. The score loses points for things the vendor controls: documentation that still describes version 1.6 while releases are on 26.x, a FAQ page that is mostly placeholders, and a hardware list you have to piece together from release notes.

What this FrSky ETHOS review covers

This FrSky ETHOS review is built on FrSky's own material: the official site, the 1.6.x update guide, the GitHub wiki and the release notes up to 26.2.0 release candidate 9. It does not report hands on flight tests. The score reflects what the vendor documents and how clearly it documents it.

ETHOS is the OS inside FrSky's color screen transmitters. Its job is the same as any radio firmware: hold model setups, mix stick inputs into channels, show telemetry and warn you about it, and talk to the RF modules. What sets it apart is that it comes from the company that makes the radio, the receivers and the RF protocols.

Features FrSky documents

M

Model types

Separate templates for airplanes, gliders, helicopters and multirotors, with category specific mixers and custom mixes.

R

Two RF modules

Internal and external modules can run at the same time, each with 24 channels.

T

Telemetry and audio

Sensor values, audio call outs and alerts, with sensors that turn red if the receiver link is lost (26.1.2).

L

Lua

Scripts for tools and widgets; 26.2.0 candidates add calls to register sensors, wizards and smart glasses.

C

Controls

Touch screen, keys or scroll wheel for every menu.

S

Simulator

Desktop simulator builds plus a simulator inside FrSky Suite 2.0.2.

FrSky Suite and updates

The desktop Suite is the strongest part of the package. Radio Information shows what is out of date; the Ethos tab writes outdated components; the RF Module tab flashes the internal module; Lua library installs scripts such as StabilizerConfig for ARCHER Plus, TD and TW stabilized receivers; and Download center fetches receiver firmware. Receivers can then be flashed over the air or through the S.Port from the radio.

Release pace is quick. The 26.2.0 line went from its first release candidate on 16 October to the ninth on 6 November, with changes such as faster startup, a Remote Control USB mode and 24 channel trainer on all connections. The flip side is that the newest builds carry an early adopters label, so the Stable branch in the Suite is the one to pick. When you are ready, the FrSky Suite download page lists the current files, and the guide on how to update the firmware covers the order of steps.

Limits

ETHOS runs only on FrSky radios. The FAQ names the TANDEM and TWIN series and the Horus X10 and X12 with ISRM; other models appear only in release notes. The supported radios list brings those names together.

Documentation trails the software. The English manual on the site is for 1.6.3, the comprehensive update guide targets 1.6.x, and the FAQ page has one answered question. FrSky also states no licence, price or support terms for the firmware. If you need a firmware that runs on several brands, read the ETHOS vs EdgeTX comparison.

Frequently asked questions

Is FrSky ETHOS good?

On FrSky's documentation it is a complete radio OS: four model type templates, two RF modules at 24 channels each, Lua and telemetry call outs. It scores 7.9 out of 10 here, with points lost for documentation that lags the firmware and for running on FrSky radios only.

Is ETHOS better than OpenTX?

FrSky's ETHOS pages do not compare it with OpenTX. The practical point is hardware: newer FrSky radios such as the X20, X18 and X14 families are built for ETHOS, and EdgeTX, the active OpenTX fork, does not list them.

Does ETHOS support helicopters?

Yes. FrSky lists helicopter as one of four model categories, alongside airplane, glider and quadcopter, each with its own mixers.

How often is ETHOS updated?

FrSky publishes releases on GitHub. The 26.2.0 line had nine release candidates between 16 October and 6 November 2026, and 26.1.2 was released on 10 September.

Can ETHOS use two RF modules at once?

Yes. FrSky states that the internal module and a module in the external bay can run at the same time, with 24 channels each.
